Use on dark or photographic backgrounds only. Apply .glass — it carries fill, blur, edge, shadow and text colour. This is the surface behind the live-call message bubbles.

Backgrounds · gradient + grain

Usage: apply .gbg + a variant as a section or panel ground; the film grain rides on ::after (SVG noise, mix-blend: overlay — soft-light on light grounds). All stops are palette tokens; white text on g1–g4, ink on g5.

Imagery · green duotone

Problem: photos on the dark stages must read as one brand, not as stock. Recipe: luminance → autocontrast → colorize into the green ramp. Dark grounds: gamma 1.08, black (4,16,10), white (86,150,112). Dim interiors get a lifted pass — gamma 1.55–1.6, white ≈ (120,190,148) — or they sink. When the sector must read true-colour (open lineup cards), keep the photo real: brightness .9 + 10% green (18,70,40) blend under a rgba(3,16,9,.32) veil. Export webp q58–66.

Light · rays & scrim

Problem: dark stages need depth without decorating over the content. Six separate streaks — never one masked sheet — slide down-right at −35°: a mint→green gradient with no white, thicker strips more blurred (13–16px), lengths peaking mid-fan. The scrim is a 210px dark ground rising from the bottom so pills and chips always sit on something. Both live under chips, bubbles and buttons.

Form fields · input types

Rules: every form on the site is composed from these four field types — same label voice, same hairline border, same radius tokens. The toggle is the only control that carries the green accent.

Buttons · quiet & wayfinding

Rules: the primary .btn stays unique per view — these are the quiet layer around it. Step navigation is named (“Next: X →”) because setup steps are independent fields, not a forced wizard; back actions are pills, not links.

One stage per industry.

The booking lineup’s stage card — a real photo of the business under the green veil, the sector chip, a live conversation with the agent, and one white “Try agent” pill. Closed sectors wait beside it as photographic tabs.

Auto Dealership

Behavior: in the lineup all cards share a fixed flex-basis and only flex-grow animates — the open card hands its room to the next in one move. It auto-rotates every 8.4s until the user touches a tab, then rotation stops for good. Every agent is Serena; conversations stagger so cards never speak in sync. State chips are hidden in the grid. “Try agent” opens the provisioning demo in an iframe popup (min(1000px, 94vh) tall). Closed tabs wear the sector photo in dark duotone under a vertical scrim; the 2×2 bento variant drops the tabs and keeps every card open.
